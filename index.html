    About MediSearch

    MediSearch is a free, open-access medicine information platform powered by the U.S. FDA OpenData API. Our mission is to make reliable drug information accessible to everyone — patients, caregivers, and healthcare professionals alike.

    Our Mission

    Democratize access to accurate, trustworthy medicine information worldwide.

    Our Vision

    A world where every person can make informed health decisions with confidence.

    Our Values

    Accuracy, transparency, accessibility, and patient safety above all else.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is MediSearch free to use?

    Yes! MediSearch is completely free. No subscription or API key required.

    Where does the data come from?

    All medicine data is sourced from the official U.S. FDA OpenData API (open.fda.gov).

    Can I use this for medical advice?

    No. MediSearch is for educational purposes only. Always consult a licensed physician.

    My medicine is not showing up?

    Try the generic name instead of brand name. Not all medicines are in the FDA database.
